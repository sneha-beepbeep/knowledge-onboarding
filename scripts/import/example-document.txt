Expense claims: quick guide

Fictional example for the question import format. Tallowmere Systems - Finance team.

Submitting a claim

Submit every expense claim in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase. Claims submitted later are only paid with written approval from your team lead.

Attach a photo or scan of the itemised receipt to each claim. A card statement on its own is not accepted as a receipt.

Approval and payment

Your team lead approves claims up to 500 euros. Claims above 500 euros also need approval from the Finance team.

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary run. Claims approved after the 20th of the month are paid in the following month.
